## Description

The app assumes exactly one project, opened from the directory you launched it
in. Everything below is what it takes to stop assuming that — plus the two
pieces that make it usable by someone who is not already a developer with a
repo and a terminal.

These are bigger swings than the Phase 2 gaps and are deliberately grouped so
they can be sequenced together rather than half-landed.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every child shipped, or explicitly deferred with a reason recorded
- [ ] The app works against more than one project without restarting it
- [ ] The phone app runs the same frontend as the desktop app
