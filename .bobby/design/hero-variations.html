PART TWO

Five grounds for the hero

The same scene five times, so only the colour changes. Whichever art wins in part one, these palettes apply to it. Every one keeps the brand red #C8412C on the button.

01 · Midnight

What's built today. Plum near-black, sampled from Super Sprint's tarmac. Quiet, and the pixel colour does all the talking.

02 · Dusk

Violet into magenta. The warmest option that still reads as evening, and it hands the page a real sky.

03 · Golden hour

Le Mans in the last hour of light. The boldest, and the one that fights hardest with the brand red.

04 · Floodlit

A cool, hard night race. Colder than today's ground, and it makes the yellow read as light rather than paint.

05 · Attract

Super Sprint's own attract screen, sampled: #600070 at 52% of the frame. The loudest, and the most obviously a game.

How to answer

Two words is enough: a number from part one and a number from part two. "01 and 03" is a complete answer. If none of the grounds is right, saying which direction to push — warmer, colder, lighter, louder — is just as useful.
