What makes railcode's band clean

I measured it rather than guessed. The answer is not "fewer ideas" — the park has a coaster, a ferris wheel, a carousel, a stall, trees and dozens of people. The answer is separation, and it comes out as numbers you can build to.

MEASURED

The 89 / 11 rule

Sampled from the live band at 1440×200. Four colours carry nearly the whole frame, and the objects are a thin garnish on top of a lot of nothing.

WHATMEASUREDWHAT IT MEANS FOR US
